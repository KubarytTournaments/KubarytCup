<h1 align="center">Ruling $04-09KC</h1>

## 1. Withdrawals

1.1 Player `Michaleklos` has withdrawn from the tournament.

## 2. Punishments

2.1 During the broadcast of Player `GumisGLHF` from the Match `Tyranuxus` vs `GumisGLHF`, a screen from the `Messenger` platform was shared, on the screen there was a message sent by Player `GumisGLHF` with the following content: "Bede Cie prosil na faze resow o pomoc, przecie na te bydle trzeba resowac jak ma samograja". In the Committee's opinion, this message is a request for help to use external help mentioned in paragraph 3). point 2.2 of the Regulations, i.e. spoilers. The Committee, during the consideration of the case, found that the Player `GumisGLHF` probably did not make the request.\
Due to this situation, the Committee decided to punish Player `GumisGLHF` with a warning. The level of punishment was influenced by the following factors:

- The Player only prepared to break the Regulations, the request was not executed,
- as the Player plays in the bottom bracket of the knockout phase, the only levels of punishment that can be applied are a warning, disqualification or ban,
- in the case of a warning, each subsequent offense of Player `GumisGLHF`, as long as it is not at the level of the lower bracket final or the knockout phase final, will result in at least disqualification.

## 3. Timetable

3.1 I determine the deadline for playing the 1/4 step 1 of the bottom bracket on 26/04/2024.

3.2 I determine the deadline for playing the 1/2 step of the upper bracket and the 1/4 step 2 of the bottom bracket on 3/05/2024.
